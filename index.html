Why it's built this way

Every mechanic here exists because the memory research says re-reading your script doesn't work.

Retrieval, not review

People who kept testing themselves held near 80% a week later; people who re-read after one success dropped to the mid-30s (Roediger & Karpicke). So the app never just shows you the text.

Cues that fade

Cold recall fails on day one because there's nothing to pull on yet. Blanking out progressively more words is what works on freshly-learned material (Fiechter & Benjamin, 2018).

Spaced toward your date

The best gap between sessions is roughly 10–30% of the time you have left (Cepeda et al.), so the schedule compresses as your date approaches — and stops the evening before, because sleep does the consolidating.

Practice under a little heat

Rehearsing under mild pressure inoculates against choking (Beilock & Carr, 2001). So the app interrupts you, times you, and argues back.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's no signup, no email, no password. Your script and your progress live in your browser's local storage on the device you're using. That also means they don't follow you to another device automatically — Setup has a transfer code you copy and paste to move everything across.

Where does my script go?

It stays in your browser. The only thing that ever leaves is what you send to Anthropic during an AI roleplay, using your own API key — that's a direct call from your browser to their API, not through us. If you never turn on roleplay, nothing leaves your device at all.

Isn't memorizing going to make me sound robotic?

Robotic is what half-memorized sounds like — when you're still hunting for the next sentence, and all your attention is on retrieval instead of the person in front of you. Once the skeleton is automatic, the thinking part of your brain is free to listen and improvise. That's the whole reason to do it.

What does the AI actually grade?

How much of your own material you covered and what you missed, how you handled the pushback, and your delivery — then three specific fixes. It scores you against the script you pasted, not against a generic idea of a good pitch.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, and it installs like an app — there's an install button in the footer of the app on Android, or use your browser's "Add to Home Screen". It works offline once installed. Use the transfer code in Setup to get your script across from your laptop.

How much does the API cost me?

You buy credits from Anthropic directly and a practice session typically costs cents rather than dollars, depending on how long you talk and which model you pick. You can set a spend cap in your Anthropic console. We never see the key or the bill.
